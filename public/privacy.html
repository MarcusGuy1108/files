Neon Legion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Neon Legion is a game you can play in a web browser or as an Android app. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your progress is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: your level, gems, upgrades, sound settings and the co-op name you choose. This is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes it.

Co-op play

When you host or join a co-op game, your device connects directly to your friend's device so the two games stay in sync. To find each other, both devices briefly contact a free public connection service run by PeerJS. As with any internet connection, that service and your friend's device can see your device's IP address while you're connected. The game sends your friend only what's needed to play together: your squad's position, size and upgrades, and the name you typed.

When the game is played inside the Claude app or claude.ai, co-op runs through that platform instead, under its own privacy terms.

What we don't do

We don't collect personal information, create accounts, show ads, use analytics or tracking, or sell or share data with anyone.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game adds features that use information differently, such as in-app purchases or ads, this policy will be updated before those features go live, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's GitHub issues page.
